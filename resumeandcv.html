Research Experience

Graduate Researcher | University of Georgia

Investigating the genetic basis of phenotypic diversity in Ipomoea through genomic and bioinformatic approaches.

  • Characterizing fast-neutron-induced genetic variation in Ipomoea purpurea and Ipomoea lacunosa.
  • Developing genomic resources through genome assembly and annotation.
  • Identifying and characterizing single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) and structural variants (SVs).
  • Integrating genomic variant data with phenotypic observations to investigate genotype–phenotype relationships.
  • Investigating candidate genes associated with plant traits and evolutionary processes.

Graduate Researcher | Master's Research

Investigated phylogenetic relationships and evolutionary diversification across moss lineages using comparative phylogenomics.

  • Analyzed transcriptomic and genomic datasets to investigate evolutionary relationships among moss groups.
  • Constructed phylogenetic trees using orthologous gene datasets.
  • Examined gene duplication and its potential role in moss evolution.
